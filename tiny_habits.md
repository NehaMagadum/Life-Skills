## Question 2

### How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P.

### Answer:

B = MAP  
where,  
M = Motivation  
A = Ability  
P = Prompt  
We can use this formula to develop new habits easily:

- M – Motivation: Have a reason or desire to perform the habit.
- A – Ability: Make the habit simple and easy to perform.
- P – Prompt: Set a reminder or connect the new habit with an existing daily routine.

## Question 3

### Why it is important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of habit? (This is the most important concept in today's topic. Whatever you celebrate becomes a habit)

### Answer:

Shine means celebrating or feeling happy and proud after completing a habit, even if it is a very small achievement.

It is important because:

1. It makes us feel successful and increases our confidence.
2. It motivates us to repeat the habit.
3. It creates a positive feeling towards the habit.
4. It helps us build consistency and develop bigger habits over time.
5. It creates success momentum, where small achievements encourage us to achieve more.

## Question 5

### What is the book's perspective about Identity?

### Answer:

According to Atomic Habits by James Clear, real behavior change starts with changing our identity, not just focusing on goals or results.

Instead of saying, "I want to read a book," we should think, "I am a reader." Similarly, instead of saying, "I want to become fit," we should think, "I am a healthy person."

## Question 6

### Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?

### Answer:

According to the book, we can make good habits easier by following these four laws:

1. Make it Obvious: Keep things visible so that you remember to perform the habit.
2. Make it Attractive: Connect the habit with something you enjoy.
3. Make it Easy: Reduce the effort required to perform the habit.
4. Make it Satisfying: Give yourself an immediate reward after completing the habit.

## Question 7

### Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?

### Answer:

According to the book, we can break bad habits by following the opposite of the four laws:

1. Make it Invisible: Remove the cues that remind you of bad habits.
2. Make it Unattractive: Focus on the negative effects of bad habits.
3. Make it Difficult: Increase the effort required to perform bad habits.
4. Make it Unsatisfying: Create consequences or accountability for repeating bad habits.
