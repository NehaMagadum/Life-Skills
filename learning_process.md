# Learning Process
### Question 1
What is the Feynman Technique?
### Answer:
The principle of the 'Feynman Technique' is that you must not fool yourself and you are the easiest person to fool.  

**Process**  

**Step 1:** Take a piece of paper and write the concept name at the top.  
**Step 2:** Explain the concept using simple language.  
**Step 3:** Identify problem areas, then go back to the source to review.  
**Step 4:** Pinpoint any complicated terms and challenge yourself to simplify them.

### Question 3
What are active and diffused modes of thinking?
### Answer:
**Active Mode:** Highly concentrate on a specific mode.  
**Diffuse Mode:** A relaxed, resting state where the mind wanders freely.

### Question 4
According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.
### Answer:
1. Deconstruct the skill.  
2. Learn enough to self correct.
3. Remove practice barriers.
4. Practice at least 20 hours.

The major barrier to skill acquisition is not intellectual it's emotional.

### Question 5
What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?
### Answer:
• Focus on learning one concept at a time with 100% concentration.  
• Practice coding regularly and implement concepts with real examples.  
• Understand concepts deeply and explain them in my own words.
