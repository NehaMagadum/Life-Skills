# Listening and Active Communication
## Quest - 1
### What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?  
**Answer** -   
- Avoid getting distracted by your own thoughts.  
- Try not to interrupt the other person.  
- Use door openers.  
- Show that you are listening with body language.  
- Takes notes during important conversation.  
- Think about what others said and make sure you are both on the same page. 

## Question - 2
### According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? (Write in your own words, use simple English)  
**Answer** -   
- Listen Carefully without interrupting others.  
- Try to understand the feelings of speaker behind their words.  
- Do not judge the speaker.  
- If anything you do not understand then ask questions to clarify.
