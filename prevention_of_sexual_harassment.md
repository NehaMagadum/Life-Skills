# Prevention of Sexual Harassment
## Question - 1
### What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?
**Answer** - 
- Unwanted touching or physical contact.
- Making inappropriate sexual comments or jokes.
- Staring or making uncomfortable gestures.
- Sending unwanted sexual messages or pictures.
- Asking for sexual favours.
- Making someone uncomfortable through repeated  unwanted attention.

## Question - 2
### What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?
**Answer** -
- Clearly tell the person to stop.
- Support the victim and make them feel safe.
- Report the incident to HR or the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC).
- Keep the incident confidential.
- Maintain evidence, such as messages or emails.
- Never ignore or encourage such behaviour.
