# Grit and Growth Mindset
## Question 1
Paraphrase (summarise) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.
## Answer - 
**Grit is passion and perseverance for very long-term goals.**   

Success is not just about being talented or intelligent. It is about working hard, staying committed, learning from failure, and never giving up on long-term goals.  

Grit is important for students who face challenges, and one method to develop grit is a 'Growth Mindset'.
